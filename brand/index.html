Brand sheet · October 2026

Kairos makes Tacit.

Say it once.

Kairos is the company. Tacit is the product people talk to. One answers when to speak, the other carries what goes unsaid.

The company
Kairos

Greek καιρός, the opportune moment, as against χρόνος, clock time. The dial is every hour; the opening is the one that matters; the point is now.

The product
Tacit

The mark is a musical rest: the written sign for a silence that means something. Tacit stays quiet until a moment is worth your attention, and when you are quiet, it understands.

Later editions

One brand first. When a group of people is actually using Tacit, it gets its own edition: Margin for finance and law, Cue for creatives. Same product, tuned defaults, its own name.
