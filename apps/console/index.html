Every framework for AI-driven interfaces answers the same question: how does a model describe a screen safely? None of them answer the one after it — how does the human accept, reject, or correct what it proposed? That is this layer. Below, you can try to break it.

01

Two pieces

One decides what may happen. The other decides when it is allowed to.

AIUIC — the commit contract

An act declares what it costs to be wrong. That, not a hand-maintained list of exceptions, decides how consent is obtained: a quiet moment for something reversible, a spoken yes for something that isn't. Repair rewinds one slot, never the whole sentence.

Hepta — seven tongues

Tourner sept fois sa langue dans sa bouche avant de parler. While you are still talking, seven candidate acts stay alive at once. The leader paints the screen immediately. None of them may commit — a half-finished sentence contains no silence to read.
