Tacit · the Stage

Stage Kit

Every answer Tacit can ask for has its own face, and every moment of an action has its own beat. These are the real components: the same code Tacit draws its card with, in every state.

Live

Say it once

One sentence, heard word by word. The card fills as you speak, each answer in its own form, then holds: silence lets a cheap thing happen, a word or a tap changes it.

Kinds of answer

Seven primitives

Pick a state on any of them. Tap a choice to fill it; tap × to empty it again. The words on screen are always the answer itself, so a screen reader hears exactly what you see.

Beats

The moments of an action

From the first reading to the receipt. Only cheap things that touch nobody else are allowed to happen on silence; everything else waits for a yes.

Surfaces

Cards that tell, not do

Same card, same way out, but nothing here commits. A follow-up like “Reply to Vincent” is handed to the conversation, where the rules above decide what silence may do.

Rules the components keep

Held in the code, not in a style guide

One card at a timeDrawing a card replaces the last one. There is no way to stack two.
A way out, always“Not that”, “Undo” or “Done” is drawn in every state. A test fails without it.
Your words above itWhat you said sits outside the card, so the reading is never confused with the hearing.
Never a spinnerNothing loops. Motion only marks a change: an answer landing, a mark drawing itself.
A guess looks like a guessA value Tacit inferred is dimmed and waits for one tap to become yours.
Two readings, never a listWhen an answer is ambiguous, the card shows the two it is deciding between.
